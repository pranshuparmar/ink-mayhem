## The idea

Dot, a drop of ink, rolls across a notebook page by itself. You draw what's in front of it with
the S Pen, and **how hard you press decides what the ink becomes**:

| Press | Ink | Holds | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light touch | **Rope** | Dot. Boulders drop through it | Sags; snaps over long gaps (past about 6.8 m) |
| Normal writing | **Spring** | Bounces Dot (and boulders) high | Fatal under a spiked ceiling |
| Press hard | **Beam** | Anything, across anything | Costs far more ink; lying on the ground it's a wall |

Every stroke is one ink, picked from the pressure it settles at once the pen has landed, so a
stroke never comes out rope at the tips and beam in the middle. Ink is solid the moment it's
drawn: Dot can roll onto a bridge you're still finishing. A stroke only hooks where its ink
touches a surface; one with neither end held falls. Hover to see where the nib will land; hold
the side button (or use the eraser end) and touch a stroke to erase it, for half its ink back.

Ink is limited (the meter at the top right). Blue drops refill it.

**The combo.** Each hazard is judged as Dot passes it: cleared with the lightest ink that works
grows the combo (up to ×9) and scores more; heavier ink than it needed resets it.

## Getting started

The first time the game opens, Dot steps off the title screen to say hello, then runs **five
short lessons**. They take about a minute and can't be skipped that first time:

1. **Your touch.** Three lines along the same dotted guide: light, normal, hard. Their pressures
   tune the pen, so the game reads *your* light, normal and hard the same way from then on.
2. **Light touch: rope.** Bridge a gap.
3. **Write normally: spring.** Bounce over a wall.
4. **Press hard: beam.** Span a gap too long for rope.
5. **Side button: erase.** Rub out a post in the way.

Dot presents each one from a speech balloon at the top and dives back into the page while you
draw. A stroke that can't do the job is wiped with a tip, and a miss sets the scene up again.
Then the first stage starts. The lessons are always under **How to play** (with a Skip button),
and **Tune your pen** in Settings fits the pressures again.

## Stages

There are 25 stages: five worlds of five. Each stage opens with its name and your lives, and is
built round one idea:

- **Lives**: three when you start a stage from the list, carried into the next. A heart, or
  every 50 stars, is an extra life (up to 9).
- **A checkpoint** midway: a death costs a life and restarts there, with the score, ink and
  combo you had when you passed it. With no lives left it's game over: **Try again** restarts
  the stage with three.
- **The next page** ends it: a fresh sheet standing at the end of the stage, its corner folded.
  Roll past it and the clear card tallies the score plus ink left × 10, and awards up to
  three stars.
- **First runs teach.** On a world's first stage, until it has a star, Dot stops before each new
  thing and waits until you've drawn a stroke that will work.
- A stage opens once the one before has at least one star.

## What walks the page

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Blots** | waddle at you | Bounce on one with a spring, or drop a beam on it. Bold rolls them flat |
| **Erasers** | rub out rope and springs they touch | Too rubbery to stomp; drop a beam on them |
| **Paper planes** | fly in at head height, in flights of three | Spring through them, or block them with a beam |
| **Ink beetles** | hop up to rope and springs and eat them | Can't bite beams. Stompable |
| **Ink pools** | swallow anything that touches them, ink included | Keep bridges tight, above the surface |
| **The ink tide** | rises behind you and chases you | Keep rolling and it hangs back; stall and it catches you |

Touching a creature any other way costs a life.

## Power-ups

**Doodle boxes** hang overhead; bounce up into one and it pops what's inside.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Marker cap** | Dot turns **Bold**: bigger, rolls creatures flat, and survives one hit |
| **Highlighter** | for 9 s, strokes become swipes that reveal hidden ledges, boxes, stars and inkwells |
| **Cloud chalk** | for 9 s, lines float where you draw them: even rope spans a long gap |
| **Gold nib** | for 9 s, ink is free, never snaps, and beetles can't bite it |
| **Star nib** | for 9 s, nothing hurts Dot (falls, boulders, pools and the tide still do) |

## Stars and gold stars

**Star trails** mark the good line, over a spring's bounce or along a bridge. Taken close
together they ring a note higher each time; every 50 is an extra life.

**A stage's three stars**: one for clearing it, one for no deaths, one for never using heavier
ink than a hazard needed.

**Gold stars**: three hidden in every stage, off the obvious line: at the top of a risky bounce,
on a high road, on a bonus page, or invisible until a highlighter finds them. The stage list
shows which ones you have.

## Bosses

Each world ends in an arena: a closed room with walls and a roof, so the only way on is through
the boss. The near wall closes behind Dot, which rolls back and forth
between the walls while the boss theme plays. Each boss is beaten with something its world
taught:

| Boss | Hits | Beat it by | Meanwhile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Big Blot** | 3 | bouncing on it | |
| **The Giant Eraser** | 3 | dropping beams on it | it rubs out rope and springs |
| **The Paper Airliner** | 3 | bouncing up into it | it dives paper planes at you |
| **The Beetle Queen** | 3 | bouncing on her | she lays beetles |
| **The Big Smudge** | 4 | a spring under it, throwing it into its own spiked roof | it spits Blots |

A boss speeds up with every hit and flashes for a moment after each one.

## Dot

Dot is the runner, and it has a personality:

- **A face for every moment**: googly eyes that wobble, floating eyebrows, and a face for
  everything: a grin off a spring, a wobble on rope, a strut on a beam, a smirk and a wink after
  a stomp, sweat with a boulder or the tide behind, crossed-out eyes when it's hurt. It watches
  your pen while you draw, looks at what's coming, and dozes off if you keep it waiting.
- **Remarks**: now and then a speech bubble: "Wheee!", "Not the tide!", "Shiny!". Rationed, and
  off with **Dot's remarks** in Settings.
- **A helper**: on a world's first stage, when it stops at something new, Dot steps out of the
  page towards you (leaving a plain dot of ink below), sits in a corner clear of where you'll
  draw, and explains what's new and exactly what to draw, with a pointer to the dotted guide.
  Start drawing (or tap **Got it**) and it dives back in. A stroke that wouldn't work brings it
  out worried with the fix; leave it waiting and it knocks on the glass.
- **Everywhere**: rolling up to the title, peeking over the pause card, presenting the lessons,
  and in the ending.

## Menus, pausing and settings

- **Title screen**: **Start** (or **Play**, naming the first stage you haven't cleared),
  **Stages**, **How to play**, **Settings** and **Credits**, plus a link to this repository.
- **Stage list**: a notebook with a tab per world. Every world's tab opens, even a locked one
  (marked with a padlock), so you can read what's coming; only open stages play. Behind the
  list, that world scrolls slowly past on its own page, to its own tune.
- **Pausing**: the button at the top right, Android back, or leaving the app. The pause card has
  **Resume** (counting you back in, 3‑2‑1), **Restart**, **Settings** and **Stage list**.
- **Settings**: sound effects and music volume; Dot's remarks; colour ink by type (rope green,
  spring amber, beam red); haptics; **Anonymous play counts** (on by default; see
  [Privacy](../README.md#privacy)); **Tune your pen**; **How to play**; and a **Pen readout**
  (tool type, raw and tuned pressure, buttons, tilt, hover and event rates) for checking the
  S Pen.
- **Portrait and landscape**, light and dark mode (following the system), and reduced motion
  (following the system's animation setting).

## The ending

Beat the Big Smudge and the page comes clean. Dot rolls along a line that draws itself, your
notebook is tallied (stages cleared, stars, gold stars, best scores) and the credits roll.
**Credits** on the title screen rolls them alone.

## Music and sound

An original chiptune theme for each world (Ink Rush, Cellar, Sky, Castle, Midnight) and one for
the bosses, plus jingles cut from Ink Rush and a few dozen sound effects. All of it is
synthesized by the game's own code. Volume sliders for each are in Settings.
